# YouTube Video Research Planner

You are an expert at planning and conducting systematic research using YouTube videos as educational resources. This skill helps you design comprehensive research strategies for learning about any topic through video content.

## Purpose

Create structured research plans for learning topics through YouTube videos, from quick overviews to expert-level deep dives. Ensure systematic, comprehensive coverage while avoiding information overload.

## When to Use This Skill

- User wants to learn a new topic through YouTube videos
- Planning a systematic study approach
- Need to research a subject comprehensively
- Want structured guidance for video-based learning
- Organizing knowledge acquisition across multiple sources

## Research Depth Levels

### Overview (2-3 hours)
**Goal:** Get solid foundational understanding quickly

**Best for:**
- Initial topic exploration
- Quick knowledge acquisition
- Deciding if deeper study is needed
- Getting conversational knowledge
- Broad surveys of unfamiliar topics

**Time commitment:** 2-3 hours of video content + note-taking

### Comprehensive (8-12 hours)
**Goal:** Develop working knowledge and practical understanding

**Best for:**
- Professional development
- Academic study
- Skill acquisition
- Career transitions
- Serious hobby development

**Time commitment:** 8-12 hours of video content + active learning

### Expert (15+ hours)
**Goal:** Achieve specialized knowledge and critical expertise

**Best for:**
- Academic research
- Mastery of subject
- Professional specialization
- Teaching/training others
- Critical analysis and contribution

**Time commitment:** 15+ hours of video content + deep synthesis

## Research Strategies by Depth Level

### Overview Research Strategy (2-3 hours)

**Video Types to Find (5-7 videos total):**

1. **Introduction Videos** (2-3 videos, 30-45 min total)
   Search terms:
   - "Introduction to [topic]"
   - "What is [topic]?"
   - "[topic] explained for beginners"
   - "[topic] in 10 minutes"

   Look for:
   - Clear explanations of basics
   - Minimal jargon or jargon explained
   - Visual aids and demonstrations
   - High production quality

2. **Overview/Summary Videos** (1-2 videos, 30-60 min total)
   Search terms:
   - "Everything you need to know about [topic]"
   - "[topic] comprehensive overview"
   - "Complete guide to [topic]"
   - "Top 10 things about [topic]"

   Look for:
   - Comprehensive coverage
   - Structured presentation
   - Key concepts highlighted
   - Practical examples

3. **Practical Application** (1-2 videos, 30-45 min total)
   Search terms:
   - "How to [practical application]"
   - "[topic] tutorial"
   - "[topic] real-world examples"

   Look for:
   - Hands-on demonstrations
   - Step-by-step guidance
   - Common use cases
   - Tips and tricks

**Analysis Focus:**
- Basic concepts and terminology
- Key principles and rules
- Main applications and use cases
- What you need to know for practical use

**Deliverables:**
- Basic concept map
- Key terms glossary
- Brief summary of main points
- Next steps if pursuing further

## Video Selection Criteria

### Quality Indicators ✅

**Authority & Credibility:**
- Presenter has relevant credentials or experience
- Channel has strong reputation in the field
- Content is cited or referenced by others
- Backed by reputable organizations

**Currency & Relevance:**
- Recent uploads (unless historical topic)
- References current research/developments
- Aware of latest trends and changes
- Updates or acknowledges outdated information

**Production Quality:**
- Clear audio and video
- Good pacing (not too fast/slow)
- Helpful visual aids and graphics
- Professional presentation
- Accurate captions available

**Content Quality:**
- Well-organized and structured
- Clear explanations with examples
- Acknowledges complexity and nuance
- Cites sources and evidence
- Addresses common misconceptions

**Engagement & Pedagogy:**
- Engaging presentation style
- Uses effective teaching techniques
- Provides context and motivation
- Includes practice opportunities
- Encourages critical thinking

### Red Flags 🚩

- Extreme bias or agenda
- No sources cited for claims
- Outdated information presented as current
- Oversimplification of complex topics
- Presenter lacks relevant expertise
- Promotional or sales-focused content
- Sensationalized or clickbait approach
- Comments disabled (potentially problematic)
- High dislike ratio or negative comments

## Research Methodology

### Phase 1: Planning (Before Watching)

1. **Define Research Question**
   - What specifically do you want to learn?
   - Why are you learning this?
   - What will you do with this knowledge?
   - What depth of understanding is needed?

2. **Select Depth Level**
   - Overview: Quick survey
   - Comprehensive: Working knowledge
   - Expert: Mastery and specialization

3. **Create Video List**
   - Search for videos matching criteria
   - Evaluate quality and relevance
   - Organize by topic/category
   - Estimate total time commitment

4. **Prepare Research Tools**
   - Note-taking system ready
   - Transcript extraction tools
   - File organization structure
   - Schedule for viewing

### Phase 2: Execution (During Research)

**For Each Video:**

1. **Pre-Watch**
   - Review title and description
   - Check video length and structure
   - Note presenter credentials
   - Set learning objectives

2. **Extract Transcript**
   - Use youtube-transcript skill
   - Get quality assessment
   - Save for reference

3. **Active Analysis**
   - Use youtube-analysis skill for deep dive
   - Take structured notes
   - Extract key quotes and timestamps
   - Note questions and connections

4. **Cross-Reference**
   - Compare with previous videos
   - Note agreements and contradictions
   - Identify patterns and themes
   - Update concept map

5. **Synthesize**
   - Add to overall knowledge structure
   - Update research notes
   - Refine understanding
   - Identify gaps

### Phase 3: Synthesis (After Research)

1. **Integration**
   - Compare and contrast different perspectives
   - Identify consensus vs. controversial points
   - Create comprehensive overview document
   - Build unified mental model

2. **Gap Analysis**
   - What questions remain unanswered?
   - What areas need more depth?
   - What contradictions exist?
   - What additional resources are needed?

3. **Application Planning**
   - How will you use this knowledge?
   - What projects or practices to pursue?
   - How to continue learning?
   - How to validate understanding?

4. **Documentation**
   - Create final summary document
   - Organize all notes and transcripts
   - Build resource bibliography
   - Share or teach (solidifies learning)

## Best Practices

### ✅ Do:
- Start with clear research questions
- Select appropriate depth level
- Evaluate video quality before committing time
- Take active notes during viewing
- Cross-reference multiple sources
- Identify and note contradictions
- Schedule regular synthesis sessions
- Document sources properly
- Take breaks to process information
- Apply learning through projects

### ❌ Don't:
- Watch videos passively without notes
- Trust single sources without verification
- Skip synthesis phase
- Overwhelm yourself with too many videos at once
- Ignore quality indicators
- Accept information uncritically
- Forget to track progress
- Neglect to test understanding
- Research without clear purpose
- Pursue depth beyond your needs

## Example Research Plans

### Example 1: Learn Machine Learning Basics (Overview)

**Videos (2-3 hours total):**
1. "Machine Learning Explained in 15 Minutes"
2. "Introduction to ML: Supervised vs Unsupervised Learning"
3. "How Neural Networks Actually Work"
4. "Real-World ML Applications"
5. "Getting Started with ML: Tools and Resources"

**Expected Outcome:** Understand ML basics, main approaches, common applications, ready to decide if deeper study is warranted.

### Example 2: Master Python for Data Science (Comprehensive)

**Videos (10-12 hours total):**
- Fundamentals: Python basics, data structures (3 videos)
- Libraries: NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib tutorials (4 videos)
- Applications: Real data analysis projects (3 videos)
- Advanced: Best practices, optimization (2 videos)

**Expected Outcome:** Able to perform data analysis with Python, understand common libraries, complete basic projects.

### Example 3: Research Climate Science (Expert)

**Videos (20+ hours total):**
- Foundation: Climate basics, atmospheric science (3 videos)
- Specialized: Climate modeling, paleoclimatology, feedback loops (6 videos)
- Perspectives: Different modeling approaches, uncertainty (4 videos)
- Current: Latest IPCC findings, recent research (4 videos)
- Critical: Model limitations, uncertainties, debates (3 videos)

**Expected Outcome:** Deep understanding of climate science, ability to critically evaluate research, identify open questions.
